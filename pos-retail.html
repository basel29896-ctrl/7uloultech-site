A counter business has different problems

A grocery doesn't need a floor plan or a kitchen screen. It needs a scanner that never misses, a catalogue that reflects real stock, and an honest answer to "did I actually make money this month?" Dukkan is built for that, not adapted down from a restaurant system.

Questions shop owners ask

Can I import my existing product list?

Yes. Dukkan imports your catalogue from Excel, mapping your columns to barcode, name, price, cost, category and stock. A shop with thousands of SKUs doesn't retype them — and you can export back to Excel any time.

Which barcode scanner do I need?

Any standard USB scanner that acts as a keyboard wedge, which covers the large majority sold. We supply the scanner as part of the package, so compatibility is settled before installation day.

Does it track supplier deliveries and what I paid?

Yes. A supplier registry plus a receive-stock flow that updates stock level and cost together — so profit reporting reflects your real cost, not an assumed one.

Can it print receipts and open the cash drawer?

Yes — ESC/POS thermal printing over Web Serial with a cash-drawer kick. Paperless completion is also supported if you'd rather not print.

My shop isn't VAT-registered. Does that work?

Yes. There's a genuine tax-free mode with the tax percentage at zero and the seller tax number left blank, alongside configurable VAT for registered businesses.

Can I stop staff seeing my profit figures?

Yes. Allowed views are enforced on the server, so a cashier account can't reach reporting endpoints even by calling them directly. You grant reports access only to the people who should have it.

How much does it cost and how long does setup take?

Pricing is quoted per shop — it depends on catalogue size, terminals, hardware and scope. No separate setup fee: setup, hosting, support and monthly change requests are included. Deployment typically runs four to eight weeks. Ask for a quote.
