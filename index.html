أنظمة نقاط البيع · POS systems

POS built for how
your floor actually runs.

We build and deploy two production point-of-sale systems: CashierPOS for hospitality — restaurants, cafés, coffee houses and shisha lounges — and Dukkan for retail — shops, groceries, minimarkets, pharmacies and more. Both are bilingual Arabic and English, issue Jordan-compliant tax receipts, and are deployed as an isolated system per client with its own database. We supply the hardware and install on site or remotely to suit the venue. Every plan includes hosting, support and monthly change requests. Pricing is quoted per venue — talk to us.

Hospitality

CashierPOS

Restaurants · Cafés · Coffee houses · Shisha lounges · Bars

  • Visual floor plans — multi-floor layouts with round, chair and wide table shapes and configurable seat counts
  • Counter mode for coffee bars — a rolling single session when there are no tables to manage
  • Kitchen and drinks displays — orders land on the right screen the moment they're sent
  • Shisha queue — track what's lit, what's waiting and what needs a change
  • Entrance ticketing — a ticket price with a free-redemption category, such as one drink included
  • Staff drink allowances — tracked by category, not argued over at closing
  • Per-floor VAT and seller identity — Article-5 compliant receipts, tax extracted from gross prices
  • LAN sync — multiple terminals stay consistent across the venue
  • Offline-first — the internet drops, the orders keep going

Retail

Dukkan

Shops · Groceries · Minimarkets · Pharmacies · Clothing · Hardware · Bookshops

  • Barcode scan-to-cart — works with any standard USB scanner, plus manual search
  • Full catalogue — barcode, price, cost, category, stock level and active status per product
  • Excel import and export — bring an existing stock list in on day one instead of typing it
  • Supplier receiving — a supplier registry and receive flow that updates stock and cost together
  • Low-stock signals — know what's running out before the shelf is empty
  • Thermal receipts — ESC/POS printing over Web Serial with cash-drawer kick, or go paperless
  • Daily history — reprint or void any invoice, by day
  • Profit reporting — revenue, expenses and profit, visible only to the roles you allow
  • VAT or tax-free — configurable, for registered and non-registered businesses alike

أسئلة شائعة · FAQ

Answers, up front.

How much does a project cost?
Every project is quoted individually — tell us the scope and we price it for your case. POS runs on a subscription that already includes hosting, support, setup and monthly change requests, with no separate setup fee. The quote is fixed before we start, so there are no surprises. Get a quote →
How long does delivery take?
A landing site ships in 1–2 weeks. Full applications and POS deployments typically run 4–8 weeks depending on scope. You see progress weekly, not at the end.
Do you supply the POS hardware?
Yes — barcode scanners, ESC/POS thermal receipt printers and terminals come as part of the package. You don't source equipment separately or hunt for compatible models.
Do you install on site?
On site or remotely, whichever suits you. We install on site across Amman and Jordan, and handle remote setup where that's faster for the client.
Is there a setup fee?
No. Setup is included in the subscription or purchase — there is no separate onboarding charge.
What are monthly change requests?
Two change requests a month are included on the base tier, with more on higher tiers — new features, content changes, tweaks. Your product keeps evolving as part of the service instead of becoming a new project each time.
Do you support Arabic?
Natively. Interfaces, receipts, content and support are bilingual Arabic/English — including RTL layouts and Jordan-compliant Arabic invoicing.
What happens after launch?
We stay. Monitoring, backups, on-call support and your monthly change requests — most of our clients have been with us since their first project.
